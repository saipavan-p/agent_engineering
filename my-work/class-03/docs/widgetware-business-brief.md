# WidgetWare Business Brief

## 1. Executive Summary

WidgetWare is an enterprise software and services company helping manufacturing and industrial-automation organizations modernize plant operations and implement governed, high-impact AI capabilities.

---

## 2. Product Offerings

WidgetWare offers three core solutions:

1. **Plant Operations Platform**: Connects plant-floor and operational systems to provide comprehensive operational visibility, process monitoring, and real-time operational insights.
2. **Industrial AI Accelerator**: A governed framework and solution set enabling industrial enterprises to identify, prioritize, and deploy enterprise-grade AI use cases across manufacturing facilities.
3. **Predictive Maintenance Suite**: Ingests machinery telemetry to assist operations and reliability engineering teams in monitoring equipment health trends and planning condition-based maintenance.


---

## 3. Target Market & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

WidgetWare focuses on enterprise manufacturing and industrial organizations meeting the following criteria:

- **Target Buyers**:
  - Plant Operations / Manufacturing: VP of Manufacturing, Director of Plant Operations, Chief Digital Officer (CDO).
  - Technology / Digital Transformation: Chief Technology Officer (CTO), VP of Digital Transformation, Head of Industrial AI.
- **Company Size**: Enterprise scale with a minimum of 5,000 employees.
- **Target Industries**: Discrete manufacturing, industrial automation, automotive manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, heavy industrial equipment, and aerospace manufacturing.
- **Excluded Industries**: Consumer retail, restaurants, and personal services.
- **Target Regions**: United States, Europe, and India.

---

## 4. Key Buying Signals

Accounts are evaluated for active operational change and investment signals:
- New leadership in AI, digital transformation, or smart manufacturing.
- Publicly announced plant modernization or Industry 4.0 initiatives.
- Strategic manufacturing data consolidation or OT/IT integration programs.
- Active hiring in industrial GenAI or digital manufacturing operations.

---

## 5. SDR Role & Process Boundaries

The SDR (Sales Development Representative) evaluation process is responsible for:
1. Comparing target account data against structured ICP criteria.
2. Collecting and classifying factual evidence with source provenance.
3. Explicitly distinguishing verified facts, derived facts, inferences, unknowns, and conflicts.
4. Identifying missing information and stopping when evidence is insufficient.
5. Preparing factual background context for account evaluation.

---

## 6. Safety & Human Approval Boundaries

To maintain data integrity and brand trust, strict boundaries are enforced:
- **No Autonomous Messaging**: The SDR agent must never send emails, direct messages, or social communication.
- **No CRM Modification**: The SDR agent must never autonomously update CRM records.
- **No Autonomous Meeting Scheduling**: The SDR agent must never schedule meetings or calendar events autonomously.
- **No Legal or Pricing Commitments**: Pricing and contract terms are strictly out of scope.
- **Mandatory Human Approval**: Any prospective outreach draft, CRM update, meeting scheduling, or external engagement requires explicit review and approval by a human sales professional.
